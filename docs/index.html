The team formula

One safe ball unlocks three aggressive swings.

  1. Fairway first.Steadiest player puts a ball in play.
  2. React, don’t preset.Only attack after you know the team is covered.
  3. Middle beats tucked.Bethpage rough and bunkers turn flags into doubles.
  4. Bogey can count.On 15, 16 and 10–12, avoid four simultaneous disasters.

Pocket rules

When in doubt at Bethpage

Rough: advance to grass; don’t force the green.

Fairway bunker: take the lip out of play first.

Long approach: front-center is a legitimate target.

Team covered: that is the moment to attack.

Yardage & pin notes

Yellow-tee hole yardages use the published forward-tee scorecard (6,223 yards). The current USGA listing is 6,207 yards, so tournament markers may differ — the marker on the tee and your rangefinder always win. “Pin vs center” comes directly from tomorrow’s supplied pin sheet; blank pin adjustments on holes 6 and 9 calculate to approximately center.

The ±20% window is based on your entered typical total distance. Hazard distances are planning estimates interpreted from course aerials and published hole guides, not surveyed carry numbers. Forced carries are called out separately.
