The team formula

One safe ball unlocks three aggressive swings.

  1. Fairway first.Steadiest player puts a ball in play.
  2. React, don’t preset.Only attack after you know the team is covered.
  3. Middle beats tucked.Bethpage rough and bunkers turn flags into doubles.
  4. Bogey can count.On 15, 16 and 10–12, avoid four simultaneous disasters.

Pocket rules

When in doubt at Bethpage

Rough: advance to grass; don’t force the green.

Fairway bunker: take the lip out of play first.

Long approach: front-center is a legitimate target.

Team covered: that is the moment to attack.
